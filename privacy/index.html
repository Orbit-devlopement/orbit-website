Privacy overview

Privacy in the ORBIT pilot.

This page is a plain-language product privacy overview, not a complete legal privacy policy. A jurisdiction-specific policy must be published before general customer onboarding.

Pilot only: Please do not invite customers based on this overview alone. Obtain legal review and publish a complete GDPR-compliant notice before commercial launch.

Information the product handles

When configured, ORBIT uses Supabase to manage accounts and private workspace data. The read-only provider connector can retrieve GitHub and GitLab repository metadata, issues, pull/merge requests, and CI status using user-supplied access tokens.

Credential handling

Provider access tokens are transmitted to a protected server function and designed to be encrypted at rest. Access tokens should never be committed to source control or placed in browser storage. Disconnecting a provider deletes the saved encrypted token, but provider-side revocation should also be performed when appropriate.

Third parties

The pilot is built on Supabase and accesses provider APIs when connected. Before launch, the operator must document the applicable service providers, legal bases, retention periods, international transfers, user rights and contact details.

Pilot contact

A verified contact address and full privacy notice will be published before any customer onboarding. No personal data is collected through this marketing site.
